About Me

Building things,
and keeping the door open.

I’m David Clark, a full-stack developer from Sussex County, Delaware. I spent four and a half years at JPMorgan Chase building and securing cloud infrastructure for a banking platform, and I now build and maintain web applications at Harvard Business Services in Lewes.

I also run Denkore Group, a small technology company on the Eastern Shore. I started it because access to technology shouldn’t depend on your age, your credentials or your background.

Access

Technology shouldn’t be gated by age, credentials or background. I try to make it feel available to everyone.

Presence

I stay in it with the people I work with, through the build and after it.

Working in the open

I write about what breaks and how I fixed it, because showing the work helps the next person.
